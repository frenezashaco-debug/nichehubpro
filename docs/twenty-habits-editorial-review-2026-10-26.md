## Editorial changes

Removed unsupported failure rates, universal discipline-versus-motivation rankings, myelination and dopamine explanations inferred from ordinary actions, claims of brain rewiring within days, fixed mindset-stability deadlines, mandatory public pledges, universal morning peaks and doubled-output promises. No worksheet is presented as therapy, exposure training, a measure of brain chemistry, a guaranteed habit protocol or a formula for profit.

Each guide has a separate purpose and authored aid: method fit, personal-project scope, routine constraints, event-versus-judgment reflection, task sequence, commitment purpose, practice feedback, missed-opportunity review, support during difficulty, realistic promises, repeat opportunities, defining success, choosing one change, proportionate habit records, interpretations, commitment sorting, goal value, obstacle separation, small-business capacity and lifestyle suitability. Examples are explicitly fictional and do not assert measured outcomes.

## Sources and limits

- [NIDDK: Changing Your Habits for Better Health](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diet-nutrition/changing-habits-better-health): general health-behaviour planning, obstacles and setbacks; not proof of every editorial worksheet or a prescription for an individual.
- [Lally et al. (2010): How are habits formed?](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674): repeated behaviours and self-reported automaticity, with substantial variation. It does not supply a reader's habit deadline, validate a streak as a measure, or validate these worksheets.
- [NIMH: Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health): general priorities, support and seeking care. Reflection exercises here are not clinical treatment.
- [WHO: Mental health at work](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-at-work): working conditions and organisational support, not a prevention or profit guarantee.
- [Getting Things Done: Official overview](https://gettingthingsdone.com/what-is-gtd/): creator description of capture, clarification and related record functions, not independent comparative efficacy evidence.
