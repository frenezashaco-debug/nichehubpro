## Sources checked and boundaries

Read current public NIMH guidance on generalized anxiety, panic disorder, depression and self-care; NHS guidance on worry, thought reframing, low mood, domestic abuse, chest pain and breathlessness; and WHO guidance on stress, mental health at work and the occupational classification of burnout. Sources are linked within the relevant guides. These are institutional information sources, not original trials validating the authored worksheets. No new efficacy statistic or treatment protocol is asserted.

Removed instant-calm promises, universal recovery timetables, unsupported biological explanations and instructions that could make support conditional on completing an exercise. The care and safety sections distinguish symptoms needing appropriate assessment from a blog's inability to diagnose them. No geographically guessed helpline number was inserted. UK-specific service details on linked NHS pages are identified where relevant.

All examples are explicitly fictional and do not claim observed treatment results. Notes and lists are optional editorial aids, not diagnostic instruments. Related articles retain distinct intents, including concern sorting versus choosing a worry response, and immediate overload versus requesting support at an upcoming event.
