# Five-guide editorial batch, 11 October 2026

## Scope and results

Reviewed the five existing guides about energy, rest guilt, focus recovery, habit formation, and neuroplasticity. Applied 40 paragraph corrections, 22 additional list/wording corrections (some repeated in a contents list), and rewrote two fictional scenarios as two-paragraph planning examples without invented successful outcomes.

- Energy: removed the two-hour breakfast contradiction, water-as-diagnosis claim, and advice that could conflict with a hypoglycemia care plan. Qualified meal, movement, posture, and stress advice. Corrected the CDC sleep reference label.
- Rest guilt: removed presumed origins, unsupported comparisons between techniques, diagnostic implications, and compulsory stillness/screen-free rest advice.
- Focus: separated laboratory switching costs from individual recovery times and removed guaranteed routine benefits and the fixed recovery-clock metaphor.
- Habits: removed deterministic reward and willpower explanations, softened universal planning claims, and removed a fictional timeline for success.
- Neuroplasticity: distinguished behavioral observation from measurement of neural pathways and linked sleep guidance directly.
