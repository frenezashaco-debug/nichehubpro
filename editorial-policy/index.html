Editorial Policy

How we make NicheHubPro practical, transparent, and responsible for readers looking for better mental wellness, focus, and daily habits.

What guides our work

Useful information needs clear standards.

Our guides are written to help readers understand a topic and take small, realistic next steps. They are not a substitute for individual medical, mental-health, financial, or legal advice.

Research and sourcing

When an article makes a factual health or wellbeing claim, we aim to support it with reputable primary or clinical sources. Depending on the topic, this includes peer-reviewed research and guidance from public-health institutions such as the NHS, NIMH, WHO, CDC, APA, and major academic medical centres.

Sources appear in the references section of articles when they are used. We do not treat a single study as proof of a universal outcome, and we avoid presenting uncertain evidence as settled fact.

Writing and review

New and substantially updated guides are checked for a clear purpose, readable language, useful internal links, and a practical takeaway. We update older material when we find that it is unclear, out of date, or does not meet our standards.

Our editorial information and article dates are available to readers. For health topics, we include a medical disclaimer and encourage professional support when symptoms are severe, persistent, or urgent.

Independence and corrections

NicheHubPro does not publish undisclosed paid health claims. If we recommend a product, service, or external resource in the future, we will make any commercial relationship clear.

Found an error or an outdated claim? Please contact us with the page URL and supporting information. We review correction requests and update material when warranted.
