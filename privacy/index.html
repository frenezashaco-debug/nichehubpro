Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026  ·  NicheHubPro

This Privacy Policy describes how NicheHubPro ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website at nichehubpro.com.

Information Processed When You Use the Site

  • Website requests: Our hosting and security providers process requests needed to deliver pages. These requests can include your IP address, browser information and the requested URL.
  • Optional analytics: Our shared Google Analytics script loads only after you choose Allow analytics. It helps us understand visits and page use; do not assume this data is anonymous.
  • Newsletter: When you submit a newsletter form, your email address is sent to our existing Brevo subscription service. Complete any confirmation steps shown by that service.
  • Contact messages: The contact form sends the name, email, subject and message you provide to Formspree so we can receive your inquiry. Do not include medical records, financial details or other sensitive personal information.

How We Use Information

We use information to deliver and protect the website, respond to inquiries, manage requested email subscriptions and understand site use when analytics is allowed.

Analytics and Your Choices

Choose Allow analytics or Decline analytics in the privacy panel. Use the Privacy choices button to change your choice later. Your choice is saved in browser local storage under nhp-analytics-choice-v1; it is not a newsletter subscription.

Declining analytics disables collection through our shared script. It does not delete previously collected data or automatically remove existing cookies. You can also use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Links from Pinterest or Medium may include campaign parameters such as utm_source. These identify a distribution campaign, not a named reader. They are used in traffic reporting when the analytics service is allowed and records them.

Google AdSense and Advertising

The site includes Google's AdSense script. Inclusion of that script does not mean Google has approved the site or that advertisements are currently being served.

When advertising is served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on the applicable consent choices and configuration. Advertising consent is separate from our optional analytics panel. That panel is not a certified advertising consent management platform.

For personalized ads to visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google requires an appropriate Google-certified consent platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Our published dashboard settings alone do not demonstrate how the message behaves for a visitor. You can also review Google's advertising settings.

Cookies, Local Storage and Service Providers

Your browser can store our analytics preference and, when permitted, analytics cookies. Advertising and subscription services may have their own cookies or storage. Browser settings let you review and clear stored data; clearing your preference may cause the analytics question to appear again.

The site uses GitHub Pages and Cloudflare for delivery and security, Google services for fonts, optional analytics and advertising integration, Brevo for newsletter forms, and Formspree for contact submissions. Information necessary for the feature you use is processed by the relevant provider.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through newsletter or contact forms. For questions about information you have already submitted, contact us using the address below and identify the relevant service without sending additional private records.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact@nichehubpro.com

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last modified" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@nichehubpro.com.
